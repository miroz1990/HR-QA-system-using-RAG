TechNova Inc. - Code of Conduct
Version: 1.8
Last Updated: December 2025

1. Professional Behavior
1.1 Employees must act ethically and professionally at all times.

2. Harassment Policy
2.1 Harassment of any kind is strictly prohibited.
2.2 Violations may result in immediate termination.

3. Confidentiality
3.1 Employees must not disclose confidential company information.
3.2 This obligation continues after employment ends.

4. Conflict of Interest
4.1 Employees must avoid situations where personal interests conflict with company interests.

5. Disciplinary Actions
5.1 Violations may result in warnings, suspension, or termination.
5.2 Refer to the Grievance Policy for dispute handling.
