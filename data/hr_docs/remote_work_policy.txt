TechNova Inc. - Remote Work Policy
Version: 2.0
Last Updated: January 2026

1. Eligibility
1.1 Remote work is permitted for eligible roles.
1.2 Approval must be obtained from the direct manager.

2. Working Hours
2.1 Remote employees must be available during core hours (10:00 AM – 3:00 PM).

3. Performance
3.1 Employees must maintain productivity standards equivalent to on-site work.

4. Equipment
4.1 The company may provide necessary equipment.
4.2 Employees are responsible for maintaining equipment.

5. Compliance
5.1 Remote employees must comply with IT Security Policy requirements.
5.2 Employees working internationally must follow local labor laws.
