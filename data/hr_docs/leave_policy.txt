TechNova Inc. - Leave Policy
Version: 2.1
Last Updated: February 2026

1. General Provisions
1.1 This policy applies to all full-time employees.
1.2 This policy overrides the Employee Handbook in case of conflict.

2. Annual Leave
2.1 Employees are entitled to 21 working days of paid annual leave per year.
2.2 Employees with more than 5 years of service are entitled to 25 days.
2.3 Annual leave must be approved by the direct manager.

3. Probation Rules
3.1 Employees on probation are entitled to a maximum of 10 days of leave.
3.2 Leave during probation requires HR approval.

4. Sick Leave
4.1 Employees may take up to 10 days of sick leave annually.
4.2 A medical certificate is required for absences exceeding 2 consecutive days.

5. Emergency Leave
5.1 Up to 3 days per year may be granted for emergencies.

6. Unpaid Leave
6.1 Unpaid leave may be granted under exceptional circumstances.
6.2 Approval is required from both the department head and HR.

7. Approval Workflow
7.1 Managers may approve leave up to 10 consecutive days.
7.2 HR approval is required for leave exceeding 10 days.

8. Conflicts
8.1 In case of conflict, this policy takes precedence over general documents.
