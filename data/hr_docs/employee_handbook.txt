TechNova Inc. - Employee Handbook
Version: 3.2
Last Updated: January 2026

1. General Provisions
1.1 This handbook applies to all employees unless otherwise specified.
1.2 In case of conflict with specific policies (e.g., Leave Policy), the specific policy takes precedence.

2. Working Hours
2.1 Standard working hours are 40 hours per week (Sunday to Thursday, 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM).
2.2 Employees may be required to work additional hours based on business needs.

3. Attendance
3.1 Employees must notify their manager of any absence.
3.2 Repeated unexcused absences may result in disciplinary action.

4. Probation Period
4.1 All new employees undergo a probation period of 3 months.
4.2 During probation, certain benefits and leave policies may be limited.

5. Leave Overview
5.1 Employees are generally entitled to 20 days of annual leave.
5.2 Refer to the Leave Policy for detailed rules and exceptions.

6. Code of Behavior
6.1 Employees must maintain respectful and professional communication.
6.2 Violations may lead to disciplinary action as outlined in the Code of Conduct.

7. Policy Updates
7.1 The company reserves the right to update policies at any time.
