TechNova Inc. - Grievance Policy
Version: 1.3
Last Updated: October 2025

1. Reporting Issues
1.1 Employees may report issues to HR confidentially.

2. Investigation
2.1 All complaints will be investigated fairly and promptly.

3. Protection
3.1 Employees will not face retaliation for reporting concerns.

4. Escalation
4.1 If unresolved, issues may be escalated to senior management.

5. Reference
5.1 This policy supports the enforcement of the Code of Conduct.
